# Family History of Arcus Cornealis

Select whether a first- or second-degree family member aged <45 years has **arcus cornealis** (a corneal lipid deposit, often called _arcus senilis_ in older adults).

## Definitions of relatives

- **First-degree relatives**: parents, siblings, children.
- **Second-degree relatives**: grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews.
