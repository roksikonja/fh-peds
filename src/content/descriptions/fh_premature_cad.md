# Family History of Premature CAD

Select the relationship to a family member who has **premature coronary artery disease (CAD)**.

## Premature CAD

Premature coronary artery disease is defined as an event occurring before:

- **55 years** in men
- **60 years** in women

## Definitions of relatives

- **First-degree relatives**: parents, siblings, children.
- **Second-degree relatives**: grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews.
