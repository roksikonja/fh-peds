# Family History of Genetically Confirmed FH

Select whether a **first-degree relative** has **genetically confirmed familial hypercholesterolemia (FH)** — i.e. a pathogenic variant in `LDLR`, `APOB`, or `PCSK9` identified by genetic testing.

## Definitions

- **First-degree relatives**: parents, siblings, children.
- **Genetic confirmation**: a positive molecular diagnosis from an accredited clinical laboratory.

Reports of "high cholesterol" without molecular testing should be captured under _Family History of High Cholesterol_, not here.
