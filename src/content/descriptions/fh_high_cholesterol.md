# Family History of High Cholesterol

Select the relationship to a family member who has **high cholesterol**.

Any mention of *"high cholesterol"* during the examination — regardless of exact lipid concentrations or lipid-lowering therapy use — is considered positive.

## Definitions

- **First-degree relatives**: parents, siblings, children.
- **Second-degree relatives**: grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews.

Any family history reported *without an exact age* is considered positive.
