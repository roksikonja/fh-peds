# Age

Enter the age **at the time of examination**, in years.

- Decimal values are accepted (e.g. `8.3` years).
- The model is validated for **children and adolescents only** — values must be between `0` and `18` years.

Age is used directly as a model feature and, together with sex, to convert raw BMI into an age- and sex-adjusted Z-score (see the BMI field).
