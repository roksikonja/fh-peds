# Family History of Premature PAD

Select the relationship to a family member who has **premature peripheral arterial / vascular disease (PAD)**.

> Vascular disease is defined as any **atherosclerotic disease other than coronary artery disease**.

## Premature vascular disease

Defined as an event occurring before:

- **55 years** in men
- **60 years** in women

## Definitions of relatives

- **First-degree relatives**: parents, siblings, children.
- **Second-degree relatives**: grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews.

Any family history reported _without an exact age_ is considered positive.
